8AI Augmentation Log

How AI was used across the assignment, and where it was wrong

Problem statement

Provide AI augmentation logs — a record of how AI was used to produce the deliverables, what it did, and (honestly) where it helped versus where a human was essential.

What we did

  • Per-deliverable record of AI usage across items 1–7, including the self-corrections (the EnsurePending visibility fix, the refusal to fake a test result before the SDK existed, the redirect to actually use the Mermaid MCP).
  • Tools recorded: Mermaid MCP for diagram validation, .NET 8 SDK installed mid-session, grounding against backend/ and the ops/ evidence packs.
  • Helped vs. human-essential split, and a list of concrete AI failure modes observed this session (validated the wrong way first, assumed tooling was present, needed evidence to overturn a prior, scored its own eval).
  • A provenance note: the log is a human-readable summary; the authoritative evidence is the committed files and git history.
